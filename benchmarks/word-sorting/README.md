# Word sorting

`word-sorting-50.jsonl`: fifty lists in the format of BIG-Bench Hard's `word_sorting` task ("Sort the following
words alphabetically: List: …", expected: the words sorted, space-separated), generated by `build.py` from the
system word list (`/usr/share/dict/american-english`, lowercase words of 3 to 12 letters, seed 0). Each list
has 12 to 20 words; in about half, a third of the words share their first two letters, so sorting needs more
than the first letter. Grade with `answer` (case and punctuation ignored). Meta-Prompting (Suzgun & Kalai 2024)
reports this task.
